At a glance

Just choose a username. No sign-up, email address, password, or Ready Player Me account is required to use the app. You select an avatar from those provided in the app.

Voice chat sends microphone audio to other participants through a communications provider. AI speaking practice sends speech for recognition and processes the resulting dialogue with AI services.

The app also uses player and session information to connect participants and saves preferences on your headset. Technical logs and service-provider records may be retained.

You can request deletion free of charge: email ales.spital@gmail.com. See the deletion instructions below.

2. Information we process

Microphone audio and voice communication
When microphone access is enabled and a voice feature is active, the app processes your microphone audio. Agora carries live voice chat to participants in your session. Microsoft Azure Speech processes audio for speech recognition during AI speaking practice. Other participants can hear what you say in voice chat.
AI dialogue and language practice
Recognized speech, conversation context, selected language, learning prompts, and AI responses are processed to understand your input and produce relevant replies. This includes OpenAI dialogue processing, Microsoft/Azure speech generation, and the app’s configured AI search or retrieval services. Dialogue may contain personal information if you say it.
Player identity and multiplayer activity
You choose a username (display name), which the app remembers on your headset and shares in multiplayer sessions. You do not register a separate app account or provide an email address or password. Behind the scenes, Unity automatically authenticates the app anonymously and assigns a player identifier for multiplayer services. The app processes that identifier, lobby/session identifiers and codes, participant membership, readiness, and connection information. Avatar movement, headset/controller poses, and object interactions are processed to represent you and synchronize the shared activity. A service-generated or “anonymous” player ID can still be personal data.
Avatars and preferences
You select from avatars provided in the app. The app processes your selection and saves avatar and practice-language preferences on your device. Avatars are loaded from app resources; the current experience does not use Ready Player Me sign-in or online avatar creation.
Technical and diagnostic information
The app and its service providers process connection, device/software, usage, error, and performance information needed to operate and troubleshoot their services. Network services receive connection information such as IP addresses. App diagnostic output can include session identifiers, AI request/response content, and error details; do not assume conversations are excluded from logs.
Support, privacy requests, and conduct reports
If you contact us or submit a report that is delivered to us, we process your contact details, the information you provide, relevant identifiers, and any accompanying evidence or report metadata to investigate and respond.

Please avoid sharing passwords, financial information, health information, or other sensitive personal details in voice chat or AI conversations. You can use a display name that does not identify you in real life.

3. Why we use this information

We process information to deliver the learning activities you request, create and join sessions, represent participants, synchronize interactions, enable voice communication, recognize speech, and generate AI replies and spoken responses. We also use relevant information to remember preferences, diagnose problems, respond to requests, address misconduct, protect the service, and meet legal obligations.

Where European data-protection law applies, our legal bases are performance of the service agreement for processing necessary to deliver requested app features; legitimate interests in operating, troubleshooting, securing, and supporting the app, balanced against your rights; and applicable legal obligations. Where processing requires consent, that consent must be obtained and may be withdrawn. A headset permission controls device access and does not replace any consent required by law.

AI replies are generated for language-learning interactions. This feature is not used by us to make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. Services and sharing

Information is transmitted to the services needed for the feature you use. Providers may process information on our behalf and may separately process service and security information under their own terms. The app does not ask you to supply an email address or register an account with these providers.

  • Unity services: automatic anonymous authentication and player identifiers (without user registration), lobby membership and session data, and Relay/network traffic for connecting participants. Unity Privacy Policy.
  • Agora: live voice audio and the channel, connection, and technical information needed to deliver communications. Agora Privacy Policy.
  • Microsoft/Azure: speech recognition, speech generation, and configured search/hosting services supporting AI interactions. Depending on the operation, these services receive audio, recognized or generated text, queries, and technical request information. Microsoft Privacy Statement and Azure Speech data documentation.
  • OpenAI: transcribed dialogue, relevant conversation context and prompts, and generated responses for AI language practice. OpenAI API data controls.
  • Meta: Meta handles platform use, Store distribution, and platform reporting under its own policies. Information included in a conduct report may be made available to us for moderation. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Other participants: participants receive information shared in the session, such as your display name, avatar, movement/interactions, and voice when transmitted. They may retain information they hear or see independently of the app.

The app does not request your email address during normal use. If you voluntarily email us for support or a privacy request, Google/Gmail processes your email address and message sent to our contact address; see the Google Privacy Policy. We may also disclose relevant information when required by law or necessary to address abuse, protect people’s rights or safety, or establish or defend legal claims. Provider links supplement this policy; you can contact us about app-related privacy requests.

5. Storage and retention

Processing a live conversation does not mean that every related record immediately disappears. Retention differs by data type and service:

  • Local preferences: your chosen username and saved avatar/language choices remain in app storage until replaced or cleared. Device backups, where enabled by the platform, may have their own retention.
  • Session and dialogue state: the app holds context while features are running and clears or replaces it as sessions and conversations change. Ending a session does not automatically delete provider records or diagnostic output.
  • Diagnostics and provider records: logs, identifiers, and request metadata may be retained for service operation, troubleshooting, security, abuse prevention, and legal obligations. Retention depends on the service, its configuration, and the type of record. This app does not make a blanket zero-retention promise.
  • Support and reports: we keep information for as long as needed to resolve the request or report and any related safety, follow-up, or legal issue. When those purposes end, we delete or anonymize information under our control unless a legal retention obligation applies.

Microsoft documents that audio submitted to its real-time speech-to-text service is processed in server memory without storage at rest. This does not cover the app’s transcripts, diagnostic output, or other services. OpenAI documents that API abuse-monitoring logs can contain prompts and responses and are generally kept for up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions; other retention depends on the endpoint and configuration. These provider descriptions are not a guarantee that all app-related data has those retention periods.

You may ask us which records can be associated with you and request their deletion using the process below.

6. Your controls

  • You can deny or revoke the app’s microphone permission in the headset’s app-permission settings. Voice chat and speech-based AI features need microphone access to receive your speech.
  • Use the voice-chat controls provided in the app. AI listening is a separate feature: entering an AI character’s interaction area starts recognition and leaving it stops that recognition path. Muting voice chat should not be treated as a control for every microphone feature.
  • To stop all app microphone activity, revoke microphone permission or close the app.
  • Choose from the avatars provided in the app and use a username that does not identify you in real life. Clearing local app data resets your saved username and preferences.
  • Only share information you intend other participants or AI services to receive. You can leave a multiplayer session at any time.

7. Request deletion of your data

All users, in every region, can request deletion free of charge. No app account is required to make a request.

Email: ales.spital@gmail.com
Subject: VR4LL 2.0 VLE — Data deletion request

Email a deletion request
  1. Tell us that you want your VR4LL 2.0 VLE data deleted. Include your chosen username and approximate dates of use if you can. A player identifier, if known, or a support/report reference may also help identify the records. If you do not know these details, contact us anyway.
  2. We may ask for limited additional information to verify that the records concern you. Do not send passwords, service credentials, or identity documents unless a specific, justified verification step is agreed.
  3. We will review the request, delete personal data under our control where required, and use the relevant provider’s deletion process for data it processes on our behalf where applicable. We will explain the outcome and any limitations within the period required by applicable law.

Device data: you can also clear the app’s local data using the headset’s app-management options, where available. Uninstalling generally removes local app storage but does not itself request deletion of cloud records or platform backups. Contact us if you need help.

Limits and exceptions: some data may need to be retained to comply with law, resolve an ongoing safety/abuse matter, or establish or defend legal claims. We will explain a relevant exception rather than silently refusing a request. We may be unable to identify a record from a display name alone, and data that has been irreversibly anonymized cannot be linked back to you. Deletion cannot remove copies independently held by other participants or records for which another platform is independently responsible; we can direct you to the relevant provider. Any applicable legal rights remain unaffected.
