Privacy policy

What I Drew Grew is a single-player mixed reality drawing game made by emindsi. This policy explains how the Quest app handles your information, how to delete your local game, and how to contact us about privacy.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Your creations and progress

The game saves your drawings, garden arrangements, progress and settings in the app's storage on your headset. Drawings include brush strokes, colours, stroke timing and derived preview images. Saved placements include positions and the links between your creations.

These files let the game bring your drawings to life and reopen your garden. Local recovery files help restore a valid save. Your saved game remains on the device until you remove it as described below.

Hands, viewing and your room

The game processes headset and hand-tracking information while you play so you can look around, draw and interact. It does not save raw head or hand-tracking recordings. Your drawing movements are saved as strokes on the drawing surface.

Passthrough is displayed by Meta's system. The game does not capture or save passthrough camera images.

If you choose Space world and grant spatial-data access, the game uses room surfaces to position your garden. It stores the garden's position and a spatial-anchor identifier to restore that location. Room scans and persistent anchors are also managed by Meta's platform services. They are separate from your drawing files.

You can use Comfort world without granting access to your room scan. Comfort world remains the alternative when room access or an anchor is unavailable.

Online services and sharing

The game does not require an emindsi account. It has no emindsi-operated cloud save, multiplayer, public drawing upload, advertising or analytics service. emindsi does not sell your drawings or game data and cannot remotely access your local saves.

The app uses Meta XR software and headset services for tracking, passthrough, room access and anchors. Meta's operation of the headset, account and platform services is covered by Meta's privacy policy. This policy does not replace Meta's policy.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any details or attachments you choose to send. We use that information to respond to support or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment information or room scans.

Email is handled using our email service provider. Its operation is covered by Google's privacy policy. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to address your request, prevent abuse and meet applicable legal obligations. You can ask us to delete app-related correspondence. If information must be retained to meet an applicable obligation, we will explain the reason in response to your request.

Delete your game data

Every player can delete their local game, in every region, without paying a fee:

  1. In the garden, open Arrange → Studio settings → Progress → Start over.
  2. Read the confirmation and hold the confirmation button. When it succeeds, the app replaces your drawings, garden, progress and settings with a new game, removes the previous game's recovery backup and clears its derived artwork images.

To remove all app-local files instead, clear the app's storage or uninstall it using the headset's system settings. This also works if you cannot open the game. Local deletion does not require contacting emindsi because emindsi has no server copy of your game to remove.

Starting over does not delete Meta's room scan or guarantee removal of persistent anchors held by platform services. Manage platform spatial data and account information through Meta's headset/account settings and privacy controls. Local game deletion and platform-data deletion are separate actions.

Privacy questions and deletion requests

Email ales.spital@gmail.com with the subject “What I Drew Grew privacy request”. You can ask a privacy question, request deletion of app-related correspondence, or get help deleting your local game data. Requests are available to all users in all regions without a fee.

We cannot remotely erase files on your headset or delete information held by Meta. We can explain which local controls to use and direct you to the relevant platform controls.
