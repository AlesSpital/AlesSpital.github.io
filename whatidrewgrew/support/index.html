Three garden shortcuts

Create opens a new drawing. Choose plants, objects or animals.
Visit lets you choose something in the garden to interact with.
Arrange gives you editing, saved drawings, movement and Studio settings.

Common questions

What do I need to play?

A Meta Quest 3 or Quest 3S with hand tracking enabled. This version uses your hands for painting, selecting and arranging. Core gameplay works offline.

How do I bring my drawing to life?

Follow the active drawing prompt, paint on the canvas, then choose Bring to life. Some creations have more than one drawing surface. Finish the required home surfaces to complete your house.

Can I leave parts of a drawing empty?

Yes. The painted parts make your creation. Empty and erased spaces stay clear on supported paper creations, including the bench. You can leave gaps or partly paint the bench's four regions; a partial bench is valid. Each of the home's six required surfaces still needs its own painted drawing.

How do I change a drawing later?

Open Arrange and choose Edit a drawing or Edit home. Saved drawings lets you find creations you have put away.

My garden or canvas is too far away.

In Comfort world, choose Arrange → Bring garden here. Canvas settings include Bring canvas here. The nearby garden panel also offers a return shortcut when the garden is out of view. In Space world, use the room retry or Comfort-world option offered by the game.

Do I have to scan my room?

No. Start in Comfort world without room access. Space world is optional and uses your headset's room data and spatial anchors. You can continue in Comfort world if room access is declined or no usable room is available.

My hands are not responding.

Check that hand tracking is enabled in the headset settings. Put down the controllers, keep your hands visible and release any held pinch before trying again. If a system menu is open, close it and let tracking return before drawing.

Where are my drawings saved?

Your drawings, garden and progress save in this app's storage on your headset. There is no emindsi cloud save or public drawing gallery. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes local files.

How can I start again or delete my game?

Open Arrange → Studio settings → Progress → Start over, then hold the confirmation button. Read the data deletion guide for the difference between your game files and platform-managed room data.

Your garden is your artwork. If you want to keep a picture before starting over, take a screenshot with the headset's capture feature.
