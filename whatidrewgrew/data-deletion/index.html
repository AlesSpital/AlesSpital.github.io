Start over inside the game

  1. Open Arrange → Studio settings → Progress → Start over.
  2. Read the confirmation. Choose Cancel to keep your garden, or hold the confirmation button to start again.
  3. After a successful reset, a new game replaces your drawings, garden, settings and progress. The previous game's recovery backup is retired and its derived artwork images are cleared.

Remove all app-local files

If you cannot open the game, use the headset's app settings to clear the app's storage or uninstall What I Drew Grew. This removes the application-scoped local files. emindsi does not operate a server copy of your garden.

Request deletion help

Email ales.spital@gmail.com with the subject “What I Drew Grew data deletion”. You can make this request from any region without a fee. We can help you use the local deletion controls and delete app-related correspondence you have sent to us, subject to any applicable retention obligations. We cannot remotely access your headset to erase its local files or delete information held by Meta.

Room scans and spatial anchors

Your headset's room scan and persistent spatial anchors are handled by Meta's platform services. Starting a new game does not delete the room scan or guarantee that every platform-held anchor is erased. Use Meta's spatial-data, account and privacy controls to manage platform data.

For the full data-handling explanation, read the privacy policy.
