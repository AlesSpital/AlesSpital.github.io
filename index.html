    The Maker Behind the Desk

    I am Aleš Spital, an XR developer, Unity creator, and digital artist from Slovenia. I love cozy, creative, simulation-style experiences and building tools that make learning feel tangible.

    Based in Velenje, Slovenia M.Sc. Computer Science

    Builder of warm, tactile experiences

    I design XR learning tools, games, and interactive demos that make complex systems feel tangible. Recent work includes ARnet (network simulations), AR heritage reconstructions, and VR4LL 2.0 language learning spaces.

    After teaching 200+ students and delivering AI workshops for schools and companies, I now focus on freelance Unity/XR work, building experiences that feel warm, useful, and grounded in real people.

    What I build

    XR apps, VR learning spaces, PC & mobile games, 3D art, and motion pieces.

    How I work

    Values
